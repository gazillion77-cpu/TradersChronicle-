Evaluation rules to compare before buying

Before you buy, compare the three things that actually decide whether you pass: the drawdown style, whether there is a daily loss limit, and the account sizes on offer.

The three drawdown styles, in plain terms

  • Live trailing: follows your highest unrealized profit, including open positions, so it rewards banking profit quickly. Used on 1-Step.
  • End-of-day trailing: only ratchets up at the session close on realized profit, so intraday spikes never move your floor. Used on End of Day and Diamond Hands.
  • Static: a fixed dollar floor set on day one that never trails, so every dollar of profit becomes permanent cushion. Used on Static Drawdown and the 100K Direct to Funded.
