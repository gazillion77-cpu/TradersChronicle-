How it works

You trade once.
Liquid journals every copy.

  1. Your tradeYou place it on your lead account.
  2. Replica CopierCopies it to every account you choose.
  3. Your accountsTradovate, ProjectX (Topstep) or Rithmic.
  4. LiquidJournalFiles each copy on its own prop account.

Connect once, and up to 90 days of past trades can come over with it.

The connection is still in testing, so we switch it on account by account for now.

Three steps, once.

  1. 1

    Get Replica Copier

    Replica Copier runs on your computer and copies each trade to your other accounts.

    Visit Replica Copier
  2. 2

    Make a code in Liquid

    Wallet > Connect > Replica Copier. Liquid shows the code once.

  3. 3

    Paste it in Replica Copier

    Copied trades then arrive when Liquid opens, when you come back to it, and every 15 minutes while it is open.

Good to know

The partnership is optional. Liquid works the same without it.

No. Liquid works on its own: log trades by hand, import a CSV, or connect a broker. Replica Copier adds the trades it copies.

Each fill it copies: the account, the contract, buy or sell, how many contracts, the price, the time, and the fees when your broker reports them. Nothing goes back to Replica Copier, and Liquid never places an order.

In your journal, like every other trade. Liquid keeps the last 90 days of fills to pair them into trades, and deletes them when you remove the code.

In Liquid: Wallet > Connect > Replica Copier > Remove the code. Replica Copier stops at once, and the trades already in your journal stay.
