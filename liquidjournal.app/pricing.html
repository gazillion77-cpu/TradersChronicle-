Before you pickBefore you pick

Is Essential free forever?
Yes. Essential costs nothing and needs no card. It has no time limit and no cap on trades.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, in Settings. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to Essential. Everything you logged stays.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Move up and you pay the difference today; the new plan starts at once. Apex to Prestige switches at the end of the period you paid for. Nothing is charged twice.
What does LIQUID do?
It takes 40% off Prestige or Apex on monthly billing, every month. Tap Use it on a plan and the code goes to checkout with you.
Monthly or annual?
Monthly with LIQUID is the lower price today: $14.99 for Prestige and $35.99 for Apex. Annual is one payment a year, $199.99 or $479.99, and needs no code.
Do broker connections cost extra?
No. A connection is free on every plan, with no limit on trades. Tradovate, Replica Copier, TradeLocker and TopstepX sync today.
